The Business Problems (Why This Project Was Built)Before developing this analytical tool, raw transactional tables masked four critical operational and strategic bottlenecks:Severe Inventory Bloat & Capital Lockup (Dead Stock):
Out of 606 available products in the master catalog, only 158 products (26.07%) generated actual sales, while 448 products (73.93%) remained completely unsold (UnSold Products), tying up warehouse capacity and working capital.Extreme Price-Tier & Geographic Concentration Risk:Price Concentration: Products priced above USD 150 generated 94.9% (USD 107.1M) of total company profits, while items priced below USD 150 contributed only 5.1% (USD 5.8M).Geographic Concentration: Just two markets—the United States (USD 36.2M) and Australia (USD 33.3M)—accounted for 61.56% of global profit, leaving European and Canadian operations under-leveraged.Time-Series Distortion & Misleading Seasonality:
High-level monthly trend lines suggested a sharp seasonal collapse starting every July (dropping from USD 32.6M in June to USD 15.0M in July). Root-cause inspection revealed this was caused by data collection ending in mid-July 2008 rather than an actual summer demand slump.Lack of Forward-Looking Pricing & Discount Visibility:
Executive leadership lacked a dynamic sensitivity model to test how promotional discounts, supply cost fluctuations, or price adjustments would impact net ROI and breakeven thresholds.🛠️ 3. What Was Done (Technical Solution & Architecture)To address these challenges, I engineered a 5-module analytical ecosystem inside Power BI:Module 1: Executive Financial OverviewBuilt dynamic KPI cards tracking Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total COGS, and Total Quantity with trend sparklines.Designed seasonal and calendar breakdowns comparing Weekday performance (71.9% / USD 81.2M) against Weekend performance (28.1% / USD 31.7M), alongside day-of-week and quarterly profit distributions (Q1: USD 32.3M, Q2: USD 39.0M, Q3: USD 18.2M, Q4: USD 23.4M).Module 2: Product & Inventory Rationalization IntelligenceFormulated dynamic DAX measures isolating Sold Products (158) vs. UnSold Products (448) across any selected timeframe or region.Evaluated profit distribution across Size Groups, Pricing Tiers (< USD 150 vs. > USD 150), Top-5 Profitable Colors, and Top-5 Hero Products.Module 3: Root-Cause Decomposition Tree & Historical MatrixImplemented an AI-assisted Decomposition Tree drilling down across 6 hierarchical dimensions:
$$\text{Total Profit} \rightarrow \text{Year} \rightarrow \text{Country} \rightarrow \text{Size Group} \rightarrow \text{Gender} \rightarrow \text{Color} \rightarrow \text{Product Name}$$Paired the tree with a 36-month granular financial matrix (2006–2008) tracking monthly Revenue, Profit, Quantity, Sold SKUs, and Unsold SKUs side-by-side.Module 4: Customer Demographics & Lifecycle SegmentationSegmented the 18.3K customer base by Gender, Marital Status, Household Size (Total Children: 0 to 5), and Age Brackets (15–25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54).Ranked top high-value VIP customers by net lifetime profit contribution.Module 5: Dynamic What-If Strategic Simulation EngineConstructed 4 independent What-If Parameters allowing decision-makers to simulate real-time shifts in:Quantity Change %Price Change %Cost Change %Discount %Engineered automated financial outputs calculating Projected Revenue, Projected COGS, Projected Profit, Projected ROI % (70.2% baseline), Max Safe Discount % (41.2% breakeven ceiling), and an automated Strategic Signal indicator.🔍 4. Core DAX Measures & Simulation Logic// 1. Unsold Products Counter (Dynamic Catalog Diagnostic)
Sold Products = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[ProductKey])

Available Products = COUNTROWS(Products)

UnSold Products = [Available Products] - [Sold Products]

// 2. What-If Projected Revenue & Profit
Projected Quantity = 
[Total Quantity] * (1 + 'Quantity Change %'[Quantity Change % Value])

Projected Revenue = 
SUMX(
    Sales,
    Sales[OrderQuantity] * (1 + 'Quantity Change %'[Quantity Change % Value]) *
    Sales[UnitPrice] * (1 + 'Price Change %'[Price Change % Value]) *
    (1 - 'Discount %'[Discount % Value])
)

Projected COGS = 
SUMX(
    Sales,
    Sales[OrderQuantity] * (1 + 'Quantity Change %'[Quantity Change % Value]) *
    Sales[UnitCost] * (1 + 'Cost Change %'[Cost Change % Value])
)

Projected Profit = [Projected Revenue] - [Projected COGS]

Projected ROI % = DIVIDE([Projected Profit], [Projected COGS], 0)

// 3. Automated Strategic Decision Signal
Strategic Signal = 
VAR CurrentMargin = DIVIDE([Projected Profit], [Projected Revenue], 0)
RETURN
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    CurrentMargin >= 0.35, "🚀 Highly Profitable - Safe to Expand",
    CurrentMargin >= 0.15, "⚖️ Moderate Margin - Monitor Cost Structure",
    CurrentMargin > 0,     "⚠️ Thin Margin - High Promotional Risk",
    "🛑 Loss Territory - Reduce Discount / Cost Immediately"
)
